## Learned User Preferences

- Prefers visual Cursor canvases for analytical and research output, not only markdown documents.
- Focus on substance of discourse (what people are saying), not metadata like post/comment counts or specific usernames.
- Wants collapsible grouped sections default-open when reviewing structured content (e.g. camp breakdowns).
- Cares about readable chart labels and layout; prefers shorter labels or redesigned charts when text is clipped or obscured.
- Expects thorough research coverage before synthesis; will call out missing source pages or incomplete scraping.
